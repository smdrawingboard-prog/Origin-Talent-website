Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers for households looking to hire, and for candidates looking to apply. Anything not covered here — reach us directly.

What roles does Origin Talent place?

We place household and family staff across ten specialties: Au Pairs, Caregivers, House Managers, Night Nurses, Chauffeurs, Babysitters, Personal Assistants (home and office), Pet Care Specialists, Private Chefs, and Tutors & Governesses.

How are candidates screened?

Every candidate we place is background-checked and reference-verified, with qualifications and work history confirmed before you meet them. Candidates give explicit consent to screening as part of their application.

How do I hire household staff through Origin Talent?

Start with the Client Staffing Enquiry form, or call or email us directly. Tell us the role, schedule and particulars your home needs; we then screen, verify and shortlist candidates fitted to your household, and the placement is confirmed under our Client Service Agreement.

How do I apply for work with Origin Talent?

Submit the Candidate Application form. You’ll be asked for your details, the role you’re applying for, and your consent — under POPIA — to our processing of your information and to reference checks and background screening.

Where is Origin Talent based, and which areas do you serve?

We’re based at First Floor, Dainfern Square, Cnr Winnie Mandela & Broadacres Drive, Dainfern, Johannesburg, and we place staff for households, families and private estates in and around Johannesburg.

How quickly will I hear back after an enquiry or application?

We aim to be in touch within 1–2 business days of receiving your enquiry or application, by your preferred contact method.

Do you place live-in and live-out staff?

Yes — placements can be live-in or live-out, and full-time, part-time or once-off/temporary, depending on what your household needs. You can specify the arrangement in the enquiry form.

How is my personal information handled?

In accordance with POPIA. Both forms require explicit consent before submission, candidates’ sensitive details stay locked until consent is given, and our Privacy Notice explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how long we keep it — enquiries and applications that don’t lead to a placement are deleted after 12 months.
