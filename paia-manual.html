Prepared in terms of section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 ("PAIA"), as amended by the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"). Date of compilation: 20 August 2026. Date of revision: 20 August 2026.

  • DIO — Deputy Information Officer

2. Purpose of this manual

This manual helps the public: check what categories of records Origin Talent holds without needing a formal PAIA request; understand how to request access to a record; find the contact details of our Information Officer; and understand what personal information we process, why, who it may be shared with, whether it's transferred outside South Africa, and what security measures protect it.

4. Guide on how to use PAIA

The Information Regulator publishes a Guide on how to use PAIA, describing the objects of PAIA and POPIA, how to request a record, available remedies (including how to lodge a complaint with the Regulator), and related matters. The Guide is available from the Regulator's website, justice.gov.za/inforeg, or on request to our Information Officer above.

7. Subjects and categories of records held

  • Client enquiries — contact details and staffing requirements submitted through our Client Staffing Enquiry form.
  • Candidate applications — identity, contact, and role-suitability details submitted through our Candidate Application form.
  • Company records — incorporation and statutory records required to operate as a registered business.

8. Processing of personal information

8.1 Purpose

To respond to enquiries and applications, assess candidates for placement, conduct reference and background screening with consent, and manage the client and candidate relationship. Full detail is in our Privacy Notice.

8.2 Categories of data subjects and information

Clients

Name, company, contact details, address, and the household staffing requirements they submit.

Candidates

Name, date of birth, South African ID number, nationality, address, contact details, next-of-kin details, and role-related qualifications and suitability information.

8.3 Recipients

  • Origin Talent staff involved in processing the enquiry or application.
  • Prospective client households considering a candidate for a specific role, once the candidate has consented to screening.
  • Screening or reference-check providers, if and when engaged for a specific verification.

8.4 Cross-border transfer

Form submissions are stored and processed using Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, which may process data on servers outside the Republic.

8.5 Security measures

Access to stored records is restricted to Origin Talent staff who need it to do their work. We rely on Google Workspace's built-in infrastructure security for the systems that store form submissions, and review access periodically.

9. Availability of this manual

A copy of this manual is available on our website (this page), at our head office for public inspection during normal business hours, to any person on request, and to the Information Regulator on request.

10. Updating this manual

Our Information Officer will update this manual on a regular basis.
