Tutors & Governesses

Personalized educational support and child development — from professionals selected for both academic ability and character.

What we place

Our Tutors and Governesses provide personalized educational support, academic guidance, and child development assistance. We carefully select professionals who can help children excel academically while fostering confidence and personal growth.

What a Tutor or Governess can take care of

Every household is different — duties are agreed with you before a placement is made. Typical responsibilities include:

  • One-on-one academic support
  • Homework help and exam preparation
  • Structured educational programmes
  • Confidence building and personal growth
  • Progress feedback to parents

How we screen

Every candidate we put forward is background-checked and reference-verified, with qualifications and work history confirmed before you meet. Matching is personal: we fit a candidate to your household rather than slotting a name into a vacancy, and we stay involved after the placement is made.

How a placement works

  • Enquire. Tell us what your home needs through the Client Staffing Enquiry form — the role, the schedule, and any particulars.
  • Screening & matching. We screen, verify and shortlist candidates fitted to your household, and introduce the ones worth meeting.
  • Placement. Once you’ve chosen, the placement is confirmed under our Client Service Agreement — and we stay in touch afterwards.
