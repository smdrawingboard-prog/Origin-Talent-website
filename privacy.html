Privacy Notice

Last updated: 20 August 2026. This notice explains how Origin Talent Agency (Pty) Ltd ("Origin Talent," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 ("POPIA").

Who we are

Origin Talent Agency (Pty) Ltd is a domestic and household staffing recruitment agency based at First Floor, Dainfern Square, Cnr Winnie Mandela & Broadacres Drive, Dainfern, Johannesburg, 2191.

Information Officer: Roger Donaldson, Director — roger@origintalent.co.za · +27 10 502 0105. Any question, correction, deletion, or objection request relating to your personal information should be directed here.

What we collect

We collect only what's needed to respond to an enquiry or process an application, submitted directly by you through our Client Staffing Enquiry or Candidate Application forms:

If you submit a Client Enquiry

Full name, company, email, mobile and alternate phone, preferred contact method, address, and details about the role you're hiring for (category, employment type, live-in/out arrangement, hours, urgency, salary range, driving requirement, language preference, days needed, duties, and any notes you add).

If you submit a Candidate Application

Full name, date of birth, South African ID number, nationality, address, province, mobile, email, next-of-kin name and number, and details about the role you're applying for (category, position type, driver's licence, transport, availability, skills, qualifications, languages, legal right to work, and physical capability).

Why we collect it, and why some of it is needed to work with us

We process this information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or application, and get in touch by your preferred method.
  • For candidates: assess your suitability for placement, conduct reference checks and background screening (see Consent below), and — if you're shortlisted — share relevant parts of your profile with a prospective client household considering you for a role. This is the core function of a staffing agency and the reason your details are collected at all.
  • Maintain records needed to run our placement and client-service process, including the Client Service Agreement once a placement is confirmed.
  • Meet our own legal and record-keeping obligations.

Fields marked required on our forms are needed to act on your enquiry or application at all; without them, we usually can't proceed. Optional fields help us match more precisely but withholding them won't stop your submission from being received.

Consent for background screening (candidates)

Reference checks, background screening, and verification of the information you provide may involve processing information POPIA treats with extra care (for example, information related to alleged criminal behaviour, where a screening check covers this). We only carry this out with your explicit consent, given via the "Screening Consent" checkbox on the Candidate Application form. You can withdraw this consent at any time by contacting our Information Officer, though this may affect our ability to consider your application further.

Who sees it

  • Origin Talent staff involved in processing your enquiry or application.
  • Prospective client households (candidates only) — as part of the placement process, once you've consented to screening and are being considered for a specific role.
  • Screening or reference-check providers, if and when we engage one for a specific verification — we'll only share what's needed for that check.
  • Google LLC, as our form-processing and storage infrastructure (see Where it's stored below). We do not sell personal information to anyone, for any reason.

Where it's stored, and cross-border processing

Form submissions are processed and stored using Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, which may process data on servers outside South Africa. Google maintains its own data-protection safeguards for this infrastructure. By submitting a form, you consent to this transfer as part of how we operate our enquiry and application process.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and applications that don't result in a placement are retained for up to 12 months from submission, then deleted. Where a placement is made, information relevant to that placement is retained for the duration of the Client Service Agreement and for as long afterward as we're required to by law.

Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct information that's inaccurate or out of date.
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer have a valid reason to keep.
  • Object to how we're processing your information, on reasonable grounds.
  • Withdraw consent (for screening, or for processing generally) at any time, without it affecting anything we did before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these, contact our Information Officer above. If you're not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to South Africa's Information Regulator: complaints.IR@justice.gov.za · justice.gov.za/inforeg.

Cookies and this website

This site itself doesn't set tracking or advertising cookies. It loads a typeface from Google Fonts, which involves a request to Google's servers to serve that font file.

Changes to this notice

We'll update the "last updated" date above if this notice changes. Significant changes affecting how we handle information you've already given us will be communicated directly where practical.
